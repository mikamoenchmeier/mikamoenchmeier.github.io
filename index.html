About

Stop 02 · Year 13 project, 2025

Self-landing model rocket

A model rocket that steers its own thrust to land upright. Designed, built and ground-tested; graded Excellence.

Point the motor’s thrust while it fires, and the rocket can keep itself upright and land instead of falling. I designed and built it as my final-year school project in 2025, tested it on the ground, and it was graded Excellence. Visiting Rocket Lab in Auckland along the way didn’t hurt.

Stop 03 · Still running

Home server

A small server at home that runs my smart home and files my paperwork automatically.

It runs my smart home and my own cloud storage, so my files stay on hardware I control. The part I’m proudest of is the document pipeline: a letter gets scanned, read, tagged and filed into the right folder without me touching it.
